Cozy time travel puzzle

Chrono Detective

The crime has already happened. Now rewind time and stop it. Watch a robbery play out, then drag the timeline back and change one small thing until the thief is caught red-handed.

Coming soon to Android and iOSPrivacy policy →

How to play

  1. 1

    Watch the crime play out.

  2. 2

    Drag the timeline to any moment. Everyone repeats exactly what they did, until you change something.

  3. 3

    Change one small thing: lock a door, ring a phone or switch off the lights.

  4. 4

    Replay and catch the thief with the loot, or inside the vault.

Features

30 cases

10 handcrafted training cases at First Chrono Bank, then 20 city files in banks, museums, jewelers and casinos.

A new scene every time

Every attempt builds a new crime scene, and every one is checked to be solvable.

Think like a detective

Guards see a whole lit room, but in the dark only what is next to them. Thieves wait until no one is watching.

Earn your badge

Up to three stars per case, from Rookie to Chief.

Hints when you are stuck

Hints show which object matters, then when to act.

Made with care

Charming characters, a smoky noir soundtrack and satisfying sounds.
