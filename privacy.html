This website

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages of this website are visited, so we can improve them. It sets cookies with a random identifier and records the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address; Google says Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We turn off Google signals and ad personalization, and Google keeps this data for no more than 14 months. The privacy policy pages of our games do not use Google Analytics, and it has no connection to the games themselves.

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Brazil, China, Quebec, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Thailand and Turkey, it only loads after you allow it. This site has no server, so we work out where to ask from your device's time zone. Elsewhere it runs unless you turn it off. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the footer. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, it stays off unless you allow it.

Your language, colour theme and privacy choice are saved in your browser's local storage and stay on your device. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security, and its fonts load from Google Fonts.
