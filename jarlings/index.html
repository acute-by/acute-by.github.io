Cozy colour-sorting puzzle

Jarlings: Sort Puzzle

Every Jarling wants to go home. Tap a jar to lift the Jarlings on top, then tap another jar to drop them in. Fill each jar with four matching friends and hear it sing.

Coming soon to Android and iOSPrivacy policy →

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a jar to lift the Jarlings on top.

  2. 2

    Tap another jar to drop them in. Jarlings only join friends of the same colour.

  3. 3

    Fill a jar with four matching Jarlings and it plays a note.

  4. 4

    Finish the level to hear the whole chord and earn up to three stars.

Features

100 levels, 10 worlds

Travel from the Candy Shop to the Space Lab. Each level is a little tougher, and every one is guaranteed solvable.

Treasure and gifts

Open a treasure chest every 10 levels and a free gift every day.

Daily Jar

A fresh puzzle every day, with a streak to keep going.

Help when you need it

Undo, Hint and Extra Jar boosters mean you are never truly stuck.

No pressure

No timers. Play at your own pace, offline, anywhere.

Colour-blind friendly

Every Jarling has its own symbol as well as its colour.
