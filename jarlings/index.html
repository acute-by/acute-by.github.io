An endless colour-sorting adventure

Jarlings: Color Sort Puzzle

Meet adorable Jarlings, solve satisfying sorting puzzles and explore 20 bright worlds where the levels never run out. Tap a jar to lift the Jarlings on top, then tap another jar to drop them in.

Available on Google Play StoreGet it on Google Play StorePrivacy policy →

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a jar to lift the Jarlings on top.

  2. 2

    Tap another jar to drop them in. Jarlings only join friends of the same colour.

  3. 3

    Fill a jar with four matching Jarlings and it plays a note.

  4. 4

    Finish the level to hear the whole chord and earn up to three stars.

Features

Endless levels, 20 worlds

The first 1,000 levels get steadily tougher, every one checked to be solvable. After that the puzzles keep coming at full challenge, with fresh colour mixes. Travel from the Candy Shop to the Aurora Sky, with a treasure chest every 10 levels.

Bonus levels

Every 5 levels, an optional bonus: small jars, move limits or mixed jar sizes, with extra coins.

A new friend every day

Feed the Jarling of the day, open a daily gift and solve a fresh Daily Jar to keep your streak going.

Help when you need it

Undo, Hint and Extra Jar boosters mean you are never truly stuck.

No pressure

No timers. Play at your own pace, offline, anywhere.

Colour-blind friendly

Every Jarling has its own symbol as well as its colour.
