Installed Jarlings from HUAWEI AppGallery? See the AppGallery version of this policy (no ads, no purchases, no data collected).

Jarlings is a free, ad-supported puzzle game made by Acute By ("we"). This policy explains what data the game and our partners process.

Data we collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings (level, coins, sound, etc.) are stored only on your device and in your Android device backup, if you have backups turned on.
  • We do not run our own servers or accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions to serve, measure and, where you consent, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the game.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your progress

Every 10 levels you can choose to share a picture of your progress. The picture is created on your device and is only sent when you pick an app to share it with; we do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation only; we never see your payment details.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads between levels. Rewarded videos stay optional.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

Jarlings is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security & retention

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We hold no personal data ourselves; Google's retention is described in its privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@fileicu.com

Changes

We'll update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above.
