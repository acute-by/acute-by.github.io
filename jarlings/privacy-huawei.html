This policy covers Jarlings: Sort Puzzle as distributed on HUAWEI AppGallery, made by Acute By ("we"). This version of the game is free, works fully offline, and has no ads and no in-app purchases. The Google Play version is covered by a separate policy.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The game does not connect to the internet and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Data we collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress and settings (level, stars, coins, sound, vibration, accessibility options) are stored only on your device. If you have device backup turned on, your phone may include them in its own backup; we cannot access that backup.
  • We do not run servers that receive data from the game.

Permissions

The game asks for no runtime permissions. It declares only Vibration, used for optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings → Vibration.

Ads and purchases

This version shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. Coins, hints, undos and extra jars are earned in the game and have no real-money value. If a future version adds ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the AppGallery listing before that version is released.

Sharing your progress

Every 10 levels you can choose to share a picture of your progress. The picture is created on your device and is only sent when you pick an app to share it with, using your phone's share menu. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Your choices

  • Turn sound and vibration on or off at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Clear the game's data in your phone's app settings, or uninstall the game, to delete all game data stored on your device.

Children

Jarlings is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Security & retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@fileicu.com

Changes

We'll update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above.
