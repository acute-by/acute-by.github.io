PDF, image and text tools that work offline

File ICU App

File ICU puts PDF, image and text tools on your phone. Every tool works without internet, and your files never leave your phone.

Coming soon to Android and iOSVisit File ICUPrivacy policy →

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a tool, or pin the ones you use most to Home.

  2. 2

    Choose your file. It is processed on your phone, even in airplane mode.

  3. 3

    Share the result, save it to Files or open it in the next tool.

  4. 4

    Continue where you left off with your recent results.

Features

PDF tools

Merge, split, compress and organize PDFs, and edit them: add text, sign, fill forms, add watermarks, redact and crop.

Convert

PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG and text, and back to PDF. Make scanned PDFs searchable in six languages.

Image tools

Compress, resize, crop, rotate and flip photos, convert JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC, and remove EXIF metadata.

Text tools

Word counter, case converter, find and replace, sort lines, compare texts and text to speech.

Made to be private

No account and no sign-up. Your files are processed on your phone and nothing is uploaded.

Free to use

A few ads on the lists, none on tool screens, and your files are never used for ads.
