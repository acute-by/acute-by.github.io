Health ICU App

28 health calculators that work offline

Health ICU puts 28 clear, fast health calculators on your phone. They all work without internet, and nothing you enter ever leaves your phone. Each result is an estimate for general information, not medical advice.

Coming soon to Android and iOS Visit Health ICU Privacy policy →

How it works

1

Pick a calculator, or pin the ones you use most.

2

Enter your values, in metric or imperial units.

3

See your result, worked out on your phone from a published formula.

4

Open any recent calculation again with the same values.

Features

Weight and body

BMI with a colour scale, ideal weight, body fat, lean body mass and waist ratios.

Calories and nutrition

Calorie needs, BMR, macros, protein, water, weight loss date and fasting windows.

Fitness

Running pace and race predictions, heart rate zones, one-rep max, calories burned and steps.

Women's health

Period and ovulation calendars, due date, conception date and pregnancy weight gain.

Sleep and converters

Bedtimes by sleep cycle, sleep debt, and blood glucose, HbA1c, cholesterol and temperature converters.

Made to be private

No account and no sign-up. A few ads on the lists, none on calculators, and nothing you enter is used for ads.
