Pixel art conveyor puzzle

Paint Parade: Pixel Puzzle

Cheerful little painters ride a conveyor belt around a blank picture. Each one splashes its colour onto the first pixel it can see in every row and column, but only if that pixel is its colour. Choose who goes next and watch a masterpiece appear.

Coming soon to Android and iOSPrivacy policy →

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a painter to send it around the picture.

  2. 2

    Painters only paint their own colour, and only what they can reach.

  3. 3

    Painters with paint left wait on the bench, so do not let it fill up.

  4. 4

    Finish the picture to frame it in your Gallery and earn up to three stars.

Features

100 pictures, 10 studios

Hearts, kittens, cupcakes, whales, rockets and many more, each tested to be solvable.

Your Gallery

Every finished picture is framed, and you can replay for a perfect score.

Rewards every day

A free gift every day, a star chest to fill and a fresh Daily Picture to paint.

Surprisingly deep

Easy to learn, with plenty to plan. Fast-forward when you know your moves.

Golden painter

Find the golden painter for a free hint.

Made for everyone

Colour-blind friendly symbols, a reduce-motion option and smooth play on older phones.
