This policy covers Mahjong Sunroom: Big Tiles (package com.acuteby.mahjongsunroom) as distributed on HUAWEI AppGallery. This version of the game is free, works fully offline, and has no ads and no in-app purchases. The Google Play version has its own policy.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The game does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not use HUAWEI Mobile Services or Google Play services.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Who we are

Mahjong Sunroom: Big Tiles is a mahjong solitaire game made by Acute By, an independent game developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@fileicu.com.

Data we collect

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, device identifiers, advertising ID, contacts, photos, files or location.
  • Your game progress and settings (boards completed, stars, plants grown in your sunroom, hints, shuffles and undos, daily boards and gifts, settings) are stored only on your device. If you have device backup turned on, your phone may include them in its own backup; we cannot access that backup.
  • We do not run servers that receive data from the game.

Permissions

The game asks for no runtime permissions. It declares only Vibration, used for optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in the game's Settings → Vibration. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or storage access.

Third parties

The game includes no third-party SDKs that collect data. It uses open-source libraries (Android Jetpack, Kotlin) that run only on your device; they are listed in the game under Settings → Open-source licenses. When you open the privacy policy or contact us from the game's Settings, the game hands over to your browser or email app; what happens there is covered by those apps' own policies.

Ads and purchases

This version shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. Hints, shuffles and undos are earned in the game and have no real-money value. If a future version adds ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the AppGallery listing before that version is released.

Sharing your garden

When you complete a garden (every 10 boards) you can choose to share a picture of your progress. The picture is created on your device and is only sent when you pick an app to share it with, using your phone's share menu. The share text contains a link to our website. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Your rights & choices

  • Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You can still contact us with any privacy request and we will reply.
  • Turn sound and vibration on or off at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Clear the game's data in your phone's app settings, or uninstall the game, to delete all game data stored on your device.

Children

Mahjong Sunroom is made for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Security & retention

Game data stays in the game's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it, and is kept until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: support@fileicu.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the date above. Material changes will be made before the version they apply to is released.
