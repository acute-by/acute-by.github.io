One Touch Survivor

One-finger arena survival

One finger. One shield. Hold the line! Enemies close in from every side and fire glowing bolts. Turn your shield to face them, block everything and send it straight back.

Coming soon to Android and iOS Privacy policy →

How to play

1

Touch and slide anywhere: your shield follows your finger.

2

Block bolts to reflect them at your enemies.

3

Block at the last moment for a PERFECT, which hits harder.

4

Pick one of three new powers after each wave.

Features

One-finger control

Choose Point or Slide control, so your thumb never has to cover the action.

Ten waves, two Wardens

Slimes, archers, dashers and bombers, then two Warden bosses with their own attack patterns.

Endless mode

Cleared the arena? Endless mode opens, so see how far you can go.

A new power every wave

Twin Shield, Prism, Orbit Blade, Frost Aura and more, so every run builds differently.

Three heroes

The sturdy Knight, the Mage whose bolts seek enemies, and the glass-cannon Ranger.

Grow stronger

Earn coins for permanent upgrades, and take on a fresh Daily Run every day.
