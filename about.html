About Acute By

Indie studio making cozy mobile games and free privacy-first web utilities
Who we are

Tiny apps. Big smiles.

Acute By makes cozy, lightweight mobile games, plus free websites and phone apps for everyday tasks. All of them respect your time and your privacy.

Acute By exists to make the kind of apps we want on our own phones: quick to open, calm to use and honest about what they do.

Our games are made for short breaks and long evenings alike, with no timers pushing you to pay. Our websites handle everyday jobs, from merging a PDF to checking your BMI, without asking you to sign up.

Our promise

Small on your phone. Big on care.

Every game and app we make follows the same four rules.

01

Light as a feather

Tiny downloads, quick starts and smooth play, even on older phones.

02

Private by default

No accounts and no sign-ups. Your progress stays on your device.

03

Fair & friendly

No energy timers and no pay-to-win. Wherever a version has ads, videos are always your choice.

04

Made for everyone

Big, clear visuals, colour-blind friendly cues and relaxed pacing.
